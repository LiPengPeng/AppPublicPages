Big Fish Eats Small Fish · Player support

Support

Stuck on a level or having trouble with controls or display? Try the tips below or contact us.

For iPhone. Minimum requirement: iOS 15. Check the App Store listing for current device compatibility.

Controls

Drag on the game screen to steer your fish. Eat smaller fish to grow and avoid larger fish. Tap the button in the upper-right corner to pause or resume.

Rewarded video

After a failure, you may choose to watch a rewarded video to continue, at most once per run. If an ad is unavailable, you can restart. Watching an ad is never required to keep playing.

Progress and devices

The game does not require an account and has no cross-device cloud save. Current progress may not be restored after changing or reinstalling a device. The first release is for iPhone; there is no Android version.

Contact us

Please include the game version, iPhone model, iOS version, and steps to reproduce the issue. Do not send passwords or unrelated personal information.
