Big Fish Eats Small Fish · Privacy

Privacy policy

No account or developer cloud save. Optional rewarded ads are provided by Google Mobile Ads SDK.

Updated October 9, 2026. This policy applies to the iPhone game “Big Fish Eats Small Fish” (大鱼吃小鱼：深海冒险).

Game data

The game does not require an account and does not provide developer-hosted saves or cross-device syncing. The developer does not receive game progress or scores through the game. Apple device backups and other system services are controlled by Apple and your device settings.

Advertising and third-party services

The game offers an optional rewarded video after a failure, at most once per run. Ads are provided by Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google may process an IP address or approximate location, device or advertising identifiers, ad views and interactions, and diagnostic or performance data to provide and measure ads, maintain its services, and prevent abuse. Actual processing can depend on your device, region, consent status, and Google service settings.

Google’s processing is subject to its Privacy Policy. See also Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

Consent and privacy choices

The game integrates Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to update advertising consent choices at launch. UMP displays a form when a published Google configuration requires one in the applicable region. The game menu shows a privacy-options entry when UMP requires it. What appears depends on the user’s region and Google’s configuration.

Support email

If you email support, we receive your email address, description, and any material you choose to attach so we can respond. Do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. For privacy questions, contact wf8.8@qq.com.

This website

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting service may process IP addresses and ordinary request logs to deliver and protect the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. This site does not load advertising, analytics, or tracking scripts.

Changes

If the game or its data practices change, we will update this page and its date. Refer to the current App Store version and the latest version of this policy.
