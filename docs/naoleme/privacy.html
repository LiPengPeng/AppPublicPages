闹了么隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 31 日

本政策适用于 iOS App「闹了么」（英文名称 Alarmy）。我们指 App Store 产品页面所列的开发者。你无需注册账号即可使用闹钟、世界时钟、秒表和计时器。

本机数据与通知

闹钟时间、标签、重复规则、铃声、游戏设置、世界时钟和应用偏好保存在设备本机。App 使用 iOS 通知功能提供提醒，通知是否送达受系统权限及设置影响。App 可向 Apple 注册通知令牌；该令牌保存在本机。开发者的基础使用统计为本机日志，不上传闹钟内容用于广告定向。

购买与广告

VIP 购买由 Apple StoreKit 处理，我们无法取得你的支付卡信息。已确认的有效 VIP 权益可移除广告。非 VIP 用户可能看到 Google AdMob 广告，并可自愿观看激励视频使用部分选项。

Google Mobile Ads SDK 及其合作方可能处理 IP 地址（可用于估算大致位置）、设备或广告标识符、广告展示和交互数据，以及性能、崩溃和其他诊断数据，用于投放和衡量广告、分析、反欺诈与改进服务。处理情况取决于系统设置、地区、你的选择和 Google 的实际服务配置。App 不请求设备的精确定位权限，也不将闹钟标签或时间作为广告定向信息发送给 Google。详见 Google 隐私政策。

在 1.0.3 及之后的版本，在适用地区，App 会通过 Google 用户意见征求表单提供广告隐私选择，并在设置中提供所需的隐私选项。iOS 还可能询问是否允许跨 App 和网站追踪；拒绝后仍可使用闹钟，广告服务可能展示不使用广告标识符的广告。你可在 iOS「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 跟踪」修改系统授权。广告加载失败时，基本闹钟功能仍可使用。

查看、删除与联系

你可在 App 内修改或删除闹钟。卸载 App 通常会移除其本机数据；Apple 和 Google 处理的数据受其各自政策和保留规则约束。若你主动发邮件给我们，我们会收到你提供的邮箱及邮件内容，用于答复和处理问题。隐私或支持问题请联系 wf9.9@qq.com。

本 App 并非专门面向儿童。我们会在数据处理方式发生变化时更新本政策。此页面由 GitHub Pages 托管；打开页面时浏览器会连接该服务。

Alarmy Privacy Policy

Updated October 31, 2026

This policy applies to the iOS app Alarmy (闹了么). “We” means the developer listed on its App Store product page. You do not need an account to use alarms, the world clock, stopwatch or timer.

On-device data and notifications

Alarm times, labels, repeat rules, sounds, game settings, world clocks and preferences are stored on your device. The app uses iOS notifications for reminders; delivery depends on system permissions and settings. The app can register a notification token with Apple and stores it locally. The developer's basic usage logs stay on the device; alarm contents are not sent for ad targeting.

Purchases and ads

Apple StoreKit processes VIP purchases; we do not receive your payment card details. Confirmed, active VIP access removes ads. Other users may see Google AdMob ads and can choose to watch rewarded videos for certain options.

Google Mobile Ads SDK and its partners may process IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device or advertising identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, and performance, crash and other diagnostic data for advertising, measurement, analytics, fraud prevention and service improvement. Actual processing depends on your region and choices, device settings and Google's configuration. The app does not request precise device location permission or send your alarm labels or times to Google for ad targeting. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Starting with version 1.0.3, where applicable, the app uses Google's consent form and provides a privacy-options entry in settings. iOS may also ask whether to allow tracking across other companies' apps and websites. Declining does not prevent alarm use; the ad service may show ads without the advertising identifier. You can change system authorization in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Basic alarm functions remain available if ads do not load.

Access, deletion and contact

You can edit or delete alarms in the app. Uninstalling the app normally removes its on-device data; data processed by Apple or Google is subject to their policies and retention rules. If you email us, we receive your address and message to respond to your request. Contact wf9.9@qq.com for privacy or support questions.

The app is not specifically designed for children. We will update this policy if our data practices change. This page is hosted by GitHub Pages; your browser connects to that service when you open it.
