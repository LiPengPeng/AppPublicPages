ShiftFlow: plan work, make room for life

Rotations, date changes, shift reminders and full backups — all offline, all on your device.

Build a rotation

Add shifts in order, such as day, day, night, night, rest, rest, choose the first date and save. Rotations can run up to 60 days with up to 20 shift types, and shifts that cross midnight are handled correctly.

Reminders and statistics

Set a reminder per shift: at its start, or 15, 30, 60 or 120 minutes before. Statistics show shift counts, total hours, workdays, rest days and unassigned days by month, year or a custom period. Wages are not calculated.

Widgets and sharing

Check today's shift from a Home Screen or Lock Screen widget, and share the month as an image when you need to explain your plans.

Your data and languages

No account is required. Shifts and notes stay on your device, and you can export a complete JSON backup. The interface supports 10 languages, light and dark appearance, larger text and reduced animations.

Release status

Release preparation is in progress; no App Store download link is available yet. Planned as a one-time paid app with no accounts, ads, subscriptions or tracking.
