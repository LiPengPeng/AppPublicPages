Privacy policy

This policy explains how ShiftFlow handles app data and information from visits to this website.

Updated and scope

Updated October 15, 2026. This policy covers the ShiftFlow iOS app and this website.

Records on your device

Shifts, rotations, date changes, notes, preferences and pre-restore safety copies stay in the local app sandbox. The developer does not receive or upload them, and there are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking SDKs. Whether an iOS device backup includes these files depends on your system settings.

Notifications

Notification permission is requested only when you enable shift reminders. Reminders are scheduled locally and contain the shift names and times you entered; they are not sent to a developer server.

Sharing, backup and email

Files are exported or shared only when you choose. Backups contain schedules and notes; month images exclude notes. Recipients and selected services handle files under their own policies. Contacting support shares the content you choose to send; avoid passwords and unnecessary personal details.

Deletion

Clearing the schedule keeps your shift templates but removes the rotation, date changes and reminders. It does not delete exported backups or safety copies. Uninstalling removes the local app container; manage system backups and external files separately.

External services

The public pages are intended for GitHub Pages hosting, whose provider may process routine request information such as IP addresses. Apple handles App Store purchases. Visit Support for privacy questions.
