Privacy policy

Updated

October 3, 2026

Local data

Shifts, rotations, date changes, notes, preferences and pre-restore copies stay in the local app sandbox. The developer does not receive or upload them. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking SDKs. System device backups may include these files depending on your settings.

Notifications

Permission is requested only when you enable shift reminders. Notifications are scheduled locally and contain your shift names and times; they are not sent to a developer server.

Sharing, backup and email

Files are exported or shared only when you choose. Backups contain schedules and notes; month images exclude notes. Recipients and selected services handle files under their own policies. Support receives the content you choose to email; avoid passwords and unnecessary personal details.

Deletion

Clear schedule keeps shift templates but removes the rotation, date changes and reminders. It does not delete exported backups or safety copies. Uninstalling removes the app container; manage system backups and external files separately.

External services

Public pages are intended for GitHub Pages hosting, whose provider may process routine request information such as IP addresses. Apple handles App Store purchases. Visit Support for privacy questions.
