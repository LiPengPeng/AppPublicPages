Support

Set a rotation

Add shifts in order in Rotation, choose the first date and schedule time zone, then save. The pattern repeats in both directions.

Change a date

Select a calendar date and tap Change to update its shift and note. Return to rotation removes that day's override and note.

Missing reminders

Check permission and coverage in the app, then iOS notification settings and Focus. Up to 60 upcoming work-shift reminders are scheduled; reopen the app to extend coverage. System delivery cannot be guaranteed.

Backup and restore

Export a complete JSON backup in Settings. Restore replaces the schedule and preserves the previous file. Back up before uninstalling or changing devices; do not repeatedly clear damaged data.

Contact
