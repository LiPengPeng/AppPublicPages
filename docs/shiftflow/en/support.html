Support

Try these answers first. If you still need help, email support.

Set a rotation

Add shifts in order in Rotation, choose the first date and the schedule time zone, then save. The pattern repeats in both directions.

Change a date

Select a calendar date and tap Change to update that day's shift and note. Return to rotation removes the override and note.

Reminders did not arrive

Check permission and coverage in the app, then iOS notification settings and Focus. Up to 60 upcoming work-shift reminders are scheduled; reopen the app to extend coverage. System delivery cannot be guaranteed.

Statistics and widgets

Statistics count hours from each shift's start and end times, exclude rest days and never calculate wages. Widgets follow the schedule saved by the app; if one looks stale, open the app once or add the widget again.

Backup and restore

Export a complete JSON backup in Settings. Restore replaces the schedule and preserves a safety copy of the previous file. Back up before uninstalling or changing devices, and do not repeatedly clear damaged data.

Contact

Include the app and iOS versions and steps to reproduce. Screenshots should exclude private schedule information.
