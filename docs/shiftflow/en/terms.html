Personal plans

ShiftFlow is a personal rotation-record tool. Entries are supplied by you and do not replace your employer's official roster. It does not calculate statutory pay, generate official holidays or connect to employer scheduling systems.

Reminders and data

Permissions, Focus, daylight saving and system settings may affect reminders. Check shifts, time zone and reminder coverage. Use a backup reminder for important plans and export your data before changing devices, uninstalling or restoring.
