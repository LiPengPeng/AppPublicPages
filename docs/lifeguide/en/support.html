Common questions

Can I use the app offline?
Bundled content, topic browsing, search, filters, and on-device bookmarks work offline. Ads load only while online and are hidden when unavailable. Opening an external source requires an internet connection.

Are ads targeted using my searches or health topics?
The app does not send on-device search terms, reading content, bookmarks, or health-topic preferences to Google as ad-targeting signals. AdChoices on an ad provides ad-choice information; where required, you can manage consent in Settings privacy options.

How do I report an ad?
Tap “Report ad” on the ad card and describe the issue in a support email.

Will my bookmarks move to a new device?
No. Bookmarks, reading status, and preferences are stored only on the current device.

How do I clear on-device records?
Open Settings in the app and choose Clear Bookmarks and Reading Records. Uninstalling the app may also remove on-device data.

How do I request an App Store refund?
Apple handles payment. Submit a refund request through your Apple purchase history or Apple’s official support website.
