High-Value Life Guide · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This policy applies to the iOS app listed as “High-Value Life Guide” on the App Store (displayed as “Life Guide” on the device). “We” means the developer identified on the App Store product page. The app is designed for offline reading; it does not require an account or provide cloud sync.

1. Information stored on your device

The app stores bookmarks, read status, your most recent reading location, appearance settings, and text-size preferences on your device so those features work. The app does not upload these records to a server we operate.

Search and filtering run on your device. The app does not send your search terms, reading history, or bookmarks to us.

2. Network access, external links, and sharing

  • Bundled content is available offline, and the app does not operate a service server. The app may load ads as described below; unavailable ads and declining a rewarded ad do not affect browsing or reading.
  • If you choose to open a cited source, iOS opens the external website. That website may process the visit under its own privacy policy.
  • If you choose to use the system share sheet, the app provides the selected recommendation’s title, summary, and content version to iOS. Information is sent to a recipient only after you select one; the recipient’s service then handles it under its own terms and privacy practices.

3. Google AdMob advertising data

The app shows native ads in separate placements on Discover, Catalog, Scenario, Favorites, and First Times lists, and at the end of articles or illustrated guides. A banner may also appear in a reserved area above the tab bar without covering content. An interstitial may appear before opening an article when the frequency condition is met. The Google Mobile Ads SDK and its advertising partners may process IP addresses (for example, to estimate general location), device or advertising identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, and performance or diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads, control frequency, prevent fraud, and improve services. Actual processing depends on the SDK, ad configuration, iOS settings, and your privacy choices. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

The first three Life Achievements can be activated for free. After that, you may choose to watch one rewarded ad to activate each additional achievement. Confirmed legacy paid users and users with the lifetime ad-removal purchase do not need to watch ads. Rewarded ads are only for achievement activation; skipping or failing to load one does not activate the achievement. You can browse all achievements and read the Life Guide without watching ads. Ad requests remain subject to Google UMP privacy choices and confirmed purchase entitlements. You can open the UMP privacy-options entry point in Settings. AdChoices provides ad-choice information; contact support to report an inappropriate ad.

After you complete the initial privacy notice and any applicable Google UMP privacy form, the app presents Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) system prompt. If you allow tracking, Google AdMob may access your advertising identifier and link ad data with data from other companies’ apps or websites for personalized advertising and ad measurement. If you decline, tracking is restricted by the system, or you do not authorize it, the app can still show ads without IDFA and all guides remain available. Declining does not limit core features. You can review or change the choice under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. ATT, Google UMP ad privacy choices, and Firebase basic analytics consent are managed separately.

Google’s SDK privacy manifest declares that Device ID may be used for third-party advertising and analytics; the App Store privacy label therefore discloses Device ID, coarse location, advertising data, product interactions, and diagnostic data. The app disables Google Mobile Ads’ Publisher first-party ID before startup. It does not send on-device search terms, guide text, bookmarks, or health-topic preferences to Google as ad-targeting signals. Google and advertising partners handle the data they receive under their respective privacy practices.

4. Basic statistics with Google Firebase Analytics

Life Guide enables Google Firebase Analytics’ basic automatic analytics only if you choose “Agree and Continue” in the initial Terms and Privacy notice. The SDK may process automatic events such as first open, session start, and engagement time, together with technical information such as an app-instance identifier, app version, operating-system version, and device model, to understand overall use. The app does not record screen-view events or send search terms, guide text, health topics, reading positions, bookmarks, or custom content events. Analytics data is not used to build AdMob or Google Ads audiences or target ads.

You can turn analytics off under “Settings → Privacy and Analytics” to stop future collection. Data already sent to Google is not automatically deleted when you turn analytics off and is handled under Google Analytics data and retention settings. Declining analytics does not limit offline content. Google processes this information as a third-party service provider; see Google’s Privacy Policy.

5. Information you send to support

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any information or files you choose to include. We use them only to respond to and handle your request. We delete support messages when they are no longer needed, unless retention is required by law. You can request deletion of personal information in a support email by contacting the same address.

6. Viewing and deleting on-device records

You can clear bookmarks and reading records in the app’s Settings. Deleting the app generally removes records stored in its container on that device. These records are not synced between devices.

7. Children’s privacy and policy changes

The app is not designed as a service specifically for children. If the app’s features or data practices change, we will update this page and its date. We will explain material changes in an appropriate place.

8. Contact

For privacy or support questions, email wf9.9@qq.com.
