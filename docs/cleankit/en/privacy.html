CleanKit Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-13 Privacy and support contact: wf9.9@qq.com

Scope

This policy applies to the CleanKit iOS app. CleanKit helps organize photos and videos you allow it to access, scan folders you choose, compress media, manage its own cache, and schedule local scan reminders.

Information the app processes

  • Photos: After you start a scan or optimization and allow photo access, CleanKit reads photo information and on-device thumbnails within your approved library. It creates candidates for similar photos, low-quality photos, screenshots, and older photos on your device. Scans and optimization do not automatically download iCloud originals that are not already on the device.
  • Photo and video previews: If you tap a thumbnail to open a preview and its original exists only in iCloud, Apple Photos may retrieve it according to your iCloud and network settings. CleanKit shows a loading state. Media is handled through iOS PhotoKit and is not sent to CleanKit servers or advertising services.
  • Videos: To find large videos, CleanKit reads authorized video resources available on this device and totals their sizes. Video compression also works only with originals available on this device. PhotoKit scan and optimization requests disable network access and do not automatically download iCloud originals. Items that cannot be read locally are skipped.
  • Folders: After you grant directory access through the iOS file picker, CleanKit reads the names, types, sizes, and modification dates of accessible items in the selected folders. File scans do not read file contents.
  • Device storage: CleanKit reads available and total storage so it can show the device’s storage status in the app.
  • Preferences and labels: Scan preferences, reminder settings, and identifiers for photos you mark are stored in this app on your device. Scan candidates are held in memory while the app is running; scan again after relaunching.
  • Temporary media files: CleanKit creates temporary copies on your device while optimizing photos or videos and removes those temporary files when processing ends. When you save a copy, it is added to the system Photos library.
  • Local notifications: CleanKit schedules recurring notifications on your device only if you turn on reminders and allow notifications.

Use, transfer, and sharing

CleanKit is free to download and use, with no account, subscription, or in-app purchase. Google AdMob native advertising supports the app. Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) updates ad privacy choices and, where applicable, presents a privacy message form.

To load and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process IP address (which can be used to estimate a device’s approximate region), approximate location, device identifiers, advertising data, product interactions, crash diagnostics, and performance data. Google and its advertising partners may use this information to serve, measure, analyze, and improve ads. The Google UMP SDK manages ad privacy choices; its current privacy manifest also lists approximate location, product interactions, and performance data for app functionality. Processing may also depend on the privacy message shown in your region, your choices, and Google’s privacy policies. Only after an IDFA explanation is published in AdMob and the corresponding release setting is enabled will CleanKit request iOS App Tracking Transparency permission after the Google UMP explanation. You can decline and still receive ads that do not use IDFA.

Advertising SDKs do not read CleanKit photos, videos, file contents, or scan candidates. CleanKit processes photos, videos, scan results, file information, device storage information, and preferences on your device; it does not send them to advertising services. CleanKit has no app server or account data.

Optional usage analytics

Firebase Analytics is off by default. On first launch, CleanKit asks whether you agree to send basic device information and app usage statistics to Google through Firebase Analytics, to understand how features are used. If you decline, photo, video, and file-organizing features remain available. CleanKit does not send photos, videos, file contents, or text you enter to analytics, and does not add custom user properties or content-level analytics events. The Firebase Analytics integration disables IDFV collection. This choice is separate from Google UMP ad privacy choices and iOS tracking permission. You can turn analytics off at any time in Settings > Privacy & Data or Settings > Scan Preferences. Turning it off stops future analytics collection. CleanKit’s Google Analytics reporting location uses the United States default. Under the default sharing settings, Google may use Analytics data for benchmarking, technical support, and account optimization; sharing data to improve Google products and services is off. The reporting location is a reporting setting and does not mean data is stored only in that region. See Google’s Privacy Policy for details about Google’s data processing.

Retention and deletion

Scan candidates are kept only in the app’s current runtime state. Preferences, reminder settings, and manual labels stay on your device until you change them, remove labels, or delete the app. iOS device backups may retain app data according to your system settings.

Photo and video deletion is submitted through the system Photos library; the system may keep deleted items in Recently Deleted. File deletion applies only to items you select and confirm and remains subject to file-provider permissions. Deleting CleanKit removes its local app data; media in your Photos library continues to be managed by the system Photos app.

Your choices

You choose when to access photos, select folders, and enable notifications. You can change photo and notification access in iOS Settings or choose not to use the related feature. Features that need a permission will not work after it is revoked.

Children’s privacy

CleanKit does not require an account. Advertising SDKs may process device and ad-interaction information as described above. The age rating is based on the app’s content and final advertising configuration.

Policy updates and contact

CleanKit will update this policy and the App Store Connect privacy disclosures if its features, advertising SDK version, or data practices change. For questions about this policy or information handling, contact wf9.9@qq.com.
