CleanKit Support

App version: 1.0 Support email: wf9.9@qq.com

Organize photos

Choose a scan category in Storage. iOS asks for photo access the first time you scan. With limited access, CleanKit only shows the media you allowed; you can change the selection in iOS photo privacy settings. To find large videos, CleanKit reads video resources available on this device and estimates their sizes. Scanning does not automatically download iCloud originals that are not on the device; items that cannot be read locally are skipped. If you tap a thumbnail to preview an original stored only in iCloud, Apple Photos may retrieve it according to your iCloud and network settings.

Delete and compress

CleanKit shows a confirmation flow before deletion. Compression first tries to create a smaller copy. If you turn off Keep Original, CleanKit requests deletion of the original only after the copy was created successfully. Results depend on the source media format, iCloud download status, and available device storage.

Choose folders

File scans cover only folders you choose through the system file picker. iOS does not let CleanKit scan or clear other apps’ private caches or data.

Photo access is unavailable

Open Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos in iOS and check CleanKit’s access. If you use limited access, make sure the photos you want to review are included.

Contact support

If these steps do not resolve your issue, contact wf9.9@qq.com and include the app version, iOS version, and steps to reproduce the issue. Do not send photos or other sensitive personal content.
