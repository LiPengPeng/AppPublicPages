Purpose and app versions

CrateNote is a personal moving and home-storage record tool. Features depend on the version installed. Version 1.0 is on the App Store; version 2.0 features will be available only if Apple approves and releases that update. The checklist is for general reminders, not professional advice or a substitute for moving arrangements or contracts.

Your data and backups

Records are stored on your device by default. Export a backup before changing devices, uninstalling, deleting, or restoring data. You choose what to share, with whom, and where to store it. Data may not be recoverable after device failure, a system restore, or loss of an external file.

Purchases

The app is a one-time paid download from the App Store, with no subscription or in-app feature purchases. Apple’s storefront shows the applicable price and handles payment, refunds, and purchase history.

Labels and service limits

Box numbers and labels such as Fragile are reminders for your own records; they do not guarantee how a carrier handles a box. Do not treat the app as professional moving or safety advice.
