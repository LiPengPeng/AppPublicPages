Usage and terms

Purpose

BoxWhere is a personal moving and storage record tool. The checklist offers general reminders, not professional advice or a replacement for moving arrangements or contracts. Box labels do not guarantee carrier handling.

Your data

Export a backup before changing devices, uninstalling, deleting or restoring data. You choose what to share and with whom. The app aims to protect local files but cannot guarantee recovery after device failures, system restores or loss of external files.

Purchases
