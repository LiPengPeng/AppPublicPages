Support

Try these answers first. If you still need help, email support.

Contact

Support email: wf8.8@qq.com. Include your device model, iOS version, app version, the issue, and steps to reproduce it. Do not send item photos, a full backup, or passwords unless they are strictly needed to resolve the issue.

Finding an item

In version 1.0, search by item name, box number, or note on the Boxes tab. Clear filters if no results appear. Newer versions can also search places and show the full place path.

Photo recognition and photos

In versions with this feature, take a photo or select one with the system photo picker, then review and edit suggestions before saving. Recognition runs on device and the original image is not kept by default. Change camera permission in system Settings.

Speech entry

Speech entry needs microphone and speech recognition permission and uses on-device recognition only. If your device does not support offline recognition, type the item or use a photo; the app does not switch to online recognition.

Moving or restoring a backup

Export a backup to Files on the old device and import it on the new one. The app asks before replacing current data and keeps a safety copy first. Unboxing clips are not in exported backups and must be recorded again on the new device.

A file cannot be read

The app protects an unreadable original file instead of overwriting it with empty data. Export the original from Settings, then restore a valid backup.

Purchases and refunds

Apple handles purchases and refunds. Check your Apple purchase history or visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request a refund.
