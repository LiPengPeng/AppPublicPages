CrateNote: know what is in every box

Number boxes and record items, quantities, notes, and optional photos. Search by item, note, or box number to know what to open.

Home storage and moving

Record items and their places, then search when you need them. Includes box lists, photo and speech entry, a place hierarchy, and local backup and restore.

Privacy and pricing

Records are stored locally by default. No account, ads, or subscriptions. A one-time in-app purchase may be offered; see the App Store for current features and pricing.
