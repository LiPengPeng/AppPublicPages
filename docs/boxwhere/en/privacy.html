Privacy policy

This policy explains how CrateNote handles app data and information from visits to this website.

Updated and scope

Updated October 15, 2026. This policy covers the CrateNote iOS app and this website. Features vary by version; this policy describes version 1.0 and the 2.0 update in preparation.

Records on your device

Items, places, notes, checklists, and photos are stored in app data on your device. The developer has no server used to collect this content and the app has no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. Whether an iOS device backup includes app data depends on your system settings.

Photos and camera

The system photo picker provides only images you select; the app does not request full photo-library access. Camera permission is requested only when you choose to take a photo. Photos you attach in version 1.0 stay on your device. Version 2.0 photo recognition runs on device; the original image is not kept by default, and you may choose to keep a compressed thumbnail of about 60 KB per item. Photos are not sent to developer servers.

Speech and video

If included in your app version, speech recognition is forced to run on device; audio is not uploaded or saved. If offline recognition is unavailable, the app asks you to type or use a photo. Unboxing clips are up to 30 seconds, stay on your device, and are not included in app-exported backups.

Exports, sharing, and deletion

Backups and PDFs you choose to export contain the selected records. Recipients or cloud file services you choose may receive that content. QR codes contain only a local record ID and do not provide remote lookup. Version 2.0 keeps deleted records for 30 days for recovery. Uninstalling removes local app data and safety copies; manage system backups and exported files separately.

External services and contact

Apple handles App Store purchases and refund requests. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub’s privacy statement says it may process IP addresses, device details, and website usage logs as service usage information. Emailing support shares the information in your message. Do not send passwords or unnecessary private information.
